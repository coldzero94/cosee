Cosee Privacy

Cosee samples process names, process identifiers, application paths, CPU time,
and memory footprint locally on your Mac. Readings are used to show app usage
and resource insights. Recent readings are held in memory and are cleared when
Cosee quits. No analytics, accounts, cloud processing, or automatic uploads are
included. Process names and resource readings are not written to diagnostic logs.

Preferences and imported custom runner data are saved in your user account,
under io.cosee.mac preferences and ~/Library/Application Support/Cosee.
Custom metrics read only the local JSON files you explicitly select.

Cosee sends a normal quit request only after you select an application and
confirm. The application may ask you to save work or decline to quit.

External links open in your browser only when you choose them. Websites have
their own privacy policies. Cosee currently has no automatic update service.

To uninstall, disable Launch at Login in Cosee settings, quit Cosee, and remove
Cosee.app from Applications. Preferences and imported data can be removed
separately.
